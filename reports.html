How the research score works

The system scores the first 200 cryptocurrencies by global market cap, then screens out known stablecoins, cash-like products, selected excluded assets and reported daily trading volume below $5 million. The resulting eligible assets are ranked for liquidity, capitalization, moderate recent momentum and estimated supply dilution. No future prices, return predictions, or unsupported tokenomics claims are generated.

Risk & limitations

Crypto markets are volatile. Reported volume, supply and prices may be incomplete or delayed. Higher scores do not indicate safe assets or expected gains.
